For those of you who know me you'd know that I've been interested in Brazilian Jui-Jitsu for some years now. I actually enrolled in classes over a year ago and enjoyed the experience (for the most part). There were a couple of incedents that caused injuries that forced me to stop going to classes. (These were injuries on top of injuries that I already had--the shoulder thing...)

Here we are in 2009 now and I've got the itch to do it again. I'm all excited an nervous at the same time. Nervous because of the injury thing, and ecxited because, well, it's Jui-Jitsu!! It's another chance to try something new; another chance to learn and grow. Oh, and it's also a killer workout as well.

I'm keeping a positive outlook on the situation. I think things will go better for me this time 'round.

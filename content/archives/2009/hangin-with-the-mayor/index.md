Just sitting around shooting the breeze with my good buddy Worth. Tonight was one of those old school 
nights spent re-installing **WINDOWS FREAKIN 98!!** He has a friend that still uses it (I can't believe 
that) on a daily basis.

Just so you know a little back story... The computer started acting funny so Worth brought it over 
for us to examine. He told his friend that he may need to re-install the OS thus wiping out all of 
the original data. His friend agreed and gave the go ahead as long as we saved his "My Documents" 
folder. Enter problem number 1.

*Windows 98 does not have native usb disk storage support!*

We decided to run a live linux cd to handle the data recovery efforts. my first choice was Knoppix 
STD... Well, it didn't recognize the disk. So I had to move on to Sabayon POD.

Sabayon Linux is a distribution based on Gentoo Linux. It has a strong emphasis towards the artistic 
aesthetic qualities of desktop computing. But I used none of that. I booted straight to the console 
and grabbed the data we needed.

I could go on and on but I won't. I'm actually too tired to continue this rant tonight. All I can say 
is that I'm glad I use Linux for my desktop and not crappy old Windows 98.

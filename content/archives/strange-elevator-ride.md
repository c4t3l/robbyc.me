summary = 'A brief encounter with a stranger in an elevator...'

I was in the elevator earlier today and a gentleman got on at the lobby. He must have been around 18 
or 19 years old and obviously nervous. I happen to work in a building that houses several radio stations 
and it just so happens that he was going to the 9th floor. This is where 97.9 the Box and Magic 102 are 
broadcast from.

This poor kid was pacing back and forth within the elevator car. He looked very nervous about something, 
I didn't ask what.

When the door opened he immediately took on a swagger and different (more confident) appearance. I 
guess we could all learn alot from this. 

I'll leave you to it!
